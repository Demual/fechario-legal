Fechario is an app by JR Soft (“we” or “us”) for keeping track of things that expire (warranties, vehicle inspections, insurance, documents, subscriptions and expiry dates) and getting reminders before they do.

1. Summary

  • What you enter and your photos stay on your phone. There is no account or sign-up, and we have no server that receives your items or your photos.
  • The app shows Google AdMob ads. To do so, Google's SDK independently collects some device data (section 4).
  • Dates are read and products are cut out by Google ML Kit on the phone itself. Photos and text never leave it, but the SDK sends Google technical usage data (section 4).
  • We do not use any analytics tool of our own.
  • We only receive what you write to us by email.

2. What data the app processes and where

The app only asks for permission to send you notifications (the reminders). It does not ask for camera, storage, location or contacts permissions: photos are taken with the system camera or chosen by you with the photo picker.

3. Legal basis

  • Performance of the service you request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR): storing what you enter, reading and cutting out the photos you choose and scheduling reminders, all on your phone.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f):
  • the technical data that ML Kit and Google's SDKs send so that they can work, be measured and have their errors fixed (section 4);
  • replying to the emails you send us.
  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a), requested through Google's form in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland:
  • for the ads SDK to store or access information on your phone;
  • and to show you personalized advertising. If you don't give it, Google may show you limited, non-personalized ads. To measure them and prevent fraud, Google and its partners rely on their legitimate interest, which you can object to in that same form.

4. Ads (Google AdMob) and third-party SDKs

Ads. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show ads and ask for your consent. These SDKs independently collect the following data and share it with Google in order to show and measure ads, carry out analytics and prevent fraud: - your IP address, from which an approximate location is inferred; - device identifiers: the advertising ID and the app set ID; - your interactions with the app and with the ads (for example, opens and taps); - diagnostics (app and SDK performance).

The app does not add anything you enter to ad requests.

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Precise geolocation. Google shows the ad consent message with wording that is common to all its advertising partners, which is why it mentions precise geolocation. This app has no location permission and does not pass your GPS location to the SDK. According to Google, its ads SDK only uses the approximate location derived from your IP address. You can refuse that use in “Manage options”.

Google ML Kit. It reads the text in your photos and, if you ask, separates the product from the background, on the phone itself. Images and text never leave the phone. For diagnostics and usage analytics, the SDK sends Google the following, in encrypted form: - device data (manufacturer, model and Android version) and app data (package name and version); - identifiers for this installation; - for the product cut-out, which runs through Google Play services, a device identifier as well; - performance metrics, image format and size, and error codes.

According to Google, it does not pass this data on to third parties. The cut-out model is downloaded by Google Play services when you install the app, even if you never use it.

Firebase Remote Config. The app includes it, switched off: it sends nothing unless it is activated. If it is ever activated, this policy will say so.

Google Play. “Rate the app” uses Google Play's ratings feature, under its own terms.

5. International transfers

Google LLC may process the data collected by its SDKs in the United States. Google LLC participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, and also uses standard contractual clauses.

Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), with the same safeguards.

6. How to withdraw your consent and control your data

  • Ads: in the app, Settings → Ad privacy options (shown where the law requires it: EEA, United Kingdom and Switzerland). There you can withdraw your consent or object to legitimate interest, as easily as you gave it. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.
  • Reminders: turn them off in your phone's notification settings or remove the reminders from each item.
  • Your data: delete each item from its page or uninstall the app, and everything is removed from your phone.

7. Backups and switching phones

From Settings → Save a backup you can create a .zip file with your data and photos and, from Export as a spreadsheet (CSV), a table with your items. Both are saved wherever you choose (for example, in your own storage or in your personal cloud, under that service's terms). We do not receive these files.

Android's cloud backup (the one in your Google account) is turned off for this app's data: none of it is uploaded. Android does keep a list of the apps you had installed in your account so it can reinstall them, but Google manages that list and it contains no data from the app.

If, while setting up a new phone, you copy your data directly from the old one, with or without a cable, the app's data (your items, their photos and your settings) may be moved to the new phone. You ask for this transfer and Android carries it out, using its own tool or your phone maker's, under their own terms. According to Android's documentation, it goes from one phone to the other without going through servers. It never goes through ours: you make the transfer with an Android feature and JR Soft receives no data.

Whether it happens depends on your Android version and phone maker (it doesn't happen on Android 8 or earlier), so we can't guarantee it. If you set up the new phone from your Google account backup without the old phone, this app's data won't come back, because it isn't in that backup.

Anything that only works on the old phone is not transferred, such as reminders already scheduled, SDK identifiers or your ad choices: on the new phone, the app will schedule the reminders again and ask you for your choice again.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 16. Personalized ads require consent that children under that age cannot give on their own in the EU (Art. 8 GDPR; 14 in Spain, Art. 7 of the LOPDGDD, Spain's data protection act).

9. Retention

  • What you enter is kept on your phone until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • The emails you send us: for as long as needed to reply to you and, at most, one year from the last reply.
  • The data collected by Google's SDKs is kept according to Google's policy.

10. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to us. Since we do not receive what you enter, you exercise most of them directly in the app (export, edit, delete). For the data collected by its SDKs, Google handles your requests at https://myaccount.google.com and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you can lodge a complaint with the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (Spain's data protection authority, www.aepd.es) or with the data protection authority of the country where you live or work, or where the problem occurred.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so in the app's release notes on Google Play and update the date above.
